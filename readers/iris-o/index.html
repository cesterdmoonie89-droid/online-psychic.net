About Iris O.

Iris O. offers mediumship sessions and dream work for people who want to feel close to someone they have lost. She describes herself as clairaudient, which means that what she perceives tends to arrive as words, phrases and sounds rather than pictures, and she is straightforward that this is her own experience and cannot be proven to anyone else.

She treats the work as a form of careful listening. Before a session she asks who you hope to hear from, what you would like to say, and whether there is anything you would rather not discuss. She sets those limits at the start so that the conversation stays within what you are comfortable with. She also tells clients that a session may not produce anything recognisable, and that silence is a legitimate outcome.

When impressions do come, Iris offers them tentatively and asks you to confirm only what you genuinely recognise. She never pushes details that do not fit, and she does not say that a loved one is angry, trapped or waiting for something from you. Her view is that anything which increases fear or obligation does not belong in a reading. What she hopes to give you is a quiet space, a chance to say what went unsaid, and sometimes a small phrase that feels familiar.

Iris also reads dreams, usually as a companion practice. She is interested in recurring images and in how a dream sits alongside what is happening in waking life, and she treats interpretation as a conversation instead of a dictionary of symbols.

Her work is spiritual and reflective, not proof of any particular belief, and it is not a substitute for grief counselling or medical care. Her listed rate is $4.60 per minute, and every new client receives a no-charge first five minutes to see whether the style feels right.

How a session with Iris goes

  1. Set the boundaries

    Iris asks who you hope to hear from and what is off limits before anything begins.

  2. Listen

    She spends time in quiet and describes only what she perceives, without pressing details.

  3. Confirm what fits

    You tell her what you recognise; whatever does not fit is simply set aside.

  4. Close gently

    The session ends with a short grounding moment, whether or not anything came through.

Iris: frequently asked questions

Can Iris guarantee a message from someone?

No. She is honest that a session may bring nothing recognisable, and she will not invent details to fill a silence.

What does clairaudient mean?

It describes someone who perceives impressions as sounds, words or phrases. It is a personal experience, not something that can be verified.

How much does a session with Iris cost?

Her listed rate is $4.60 per minute. The first five minutes with a new reader are a no-charge introduction.

Is mediumship a substitute for counselling?

No. If grief feels overwhelming, please also speak with a doctor, a therapist or a local support service.
