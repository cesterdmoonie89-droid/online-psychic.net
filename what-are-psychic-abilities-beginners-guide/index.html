What Are Psychic Abilities? A Beginner’s Guide to Clairvoyance, Clairaudience, and More

Marcus Reid · March 13, 2026 · 6 min read

If you are new to the world of psychic readings, the terminology can feel overwhelming. Clairvoyance, clairaudience, clairsentience, claircognisance – these words sound exotic and mysterious, but the abilities they describe are actually quite straightforward once you understand the basics.

This guide breaks down the most common types of psychic abilities in clear, accessible language – so you know exactly what your psychic means when they describe how they receive information.

The Core Concept: Extra-Sensory Perception

All psychic abilities fall under the umbrella of extra-sensory perception, or ESP. This simply means the ability to perceive information through channels beyond the five ordinary senses of sight, hearing, touch, taste, and smell.

A psychic receives information about your life, your energy, and your circumstances through these extra-sensory channels. The specific channel varies from psychic to psychic – most have one or two primary modes of perception, though many use several.

Understanding which abilities your psychic uses helps you interpret how they deliver information. A clairvoyant describes images. A clairaudient relays words. A clairsentient shares feelings. Same gift, different packaging.

Clairvoyance – Clear Seeing

Clairvoyance is the most commonly known psychic ability. It involves receiving visual information – images, scenes, symbols, or visions – through the mind’s eye rather than through physical sight.

A clairvoyant psychic might see images of people, places, or events related to your question. They might receive symbolic visions – a door opening (new opportunity), a bridge (transition), a storm clearing (resolution of difficulty). They might see scenes from your past, present, or potential future.

When a clairvoyant shares what they see, it often sounds like a description of a mental film. They might say something like “I see you in an office environment, and there is a woman with red hair who plays a significant role” or “I am seeing a garden, which I interpret as growth and new beginnings in your life.”

Clairvoyance is particularly effective in readings where visual detail matters – identifying people, describing places, and seeing the progression of events.

Clairaudience – Clear Hearing

Clairaudience is the ability to hear information that is not coming through physical sound. A clairaudient psychic perceives words, names, phrases, sounds, or even music through an inner hearing channel.

This ability is especially common among mediums, who may hear the voices of deceased individuals – their names, specific phrases they used in life, or messages they wish to communicate. But clairaudience is not limited to mediumship. Clairaudient psychics may also hear guidance about your life situation in the form of words or phrases.

When a clairaudient shares information, it often includes specific words or names. They might say “I am hearing the name Margaret” or “The word I keep hearing is patience – this situation requires patience.” The specificity of clairaudient information – actual names, phrases, and words – can be particularly evidential.

Clairsentience – Clear Feeling

Clairsentience is the ability to perceive information through physical sensation or emotional feeling. A clairsentient psychic literally feels what is relevant to your reading – emotions, physical sensations, or the energy of a situation.

This is one of the most common psychic abilities, though it often goes unrecognised because it operates through the body rather than through mental imagery or inner hearing.

A clairsentient psychic might feel your emotional state without you telling them. They might experience physical sensations that correspond to your health or to a deceased loved one’s condition. They might sense the energy of a situation – whether it is positive, negative, stagnant, or dynamic.

When a clairsentient shares their perception, it sounds like emotional or physical description. They might say “I feel heaviness in the chest area – there is grief or sadness connected to this situation” or “The energy around this new opportunity feels expansive and exciting.”

Claircognisance – Clear Knowing

Claircognisance is perhaps the most difficult psychic ability to explain because it does not arrive through any sensory channel – visual, auditory, or physical. It is the experience of simply knowing something without any logical basis for that knowledge.

A claircognisant psychic receives information as direct, immediate knowledge. They do not see it, hear it, or feel it – they just know it. This might sound vague, but in practice, claircognisant insights are often startlingly specific.

A claircognisant psychic might suddenly know your partner’s name, the cause of a conflict, or the timing of an upcoming event – without being able to explain how they accessed that information. The knowledge simply arrives, fully formed and specific.

How Psychics Use Multiple Abilities

Most psychics do not rely on a single ability. They have a primary channel – the mode through which information arrives most strongly and frequently – but they also receive impressions through secondary channels.

A primarily clairvoyant psychic might also experience clairsentient flashes of emotion. A primarily clairaudient medium might also see visual images. The combination of abilities often produces richer, more detailed readings than any single channel alone.

Some psychics consciously develop secondary abilities over time. Others find that different abilities activate depending on the type of reading or the energy of the client. This variability is normal and does not indicate inconsistency – it reflects the dynamic, organic nature of psychic perception.

Psychic Tools: Tarot, Astrology, and Others

In addition to their natural abilities, many psychics use divination tools – tarot cards, astrology charts, runes, pendulums, crystals, or oracle cards. These tools are not psychic in themselves. They serve as focusing mechanisms that help the psychic access and articulate their intuitive perception.

A tarot reader, for example, uses the symbolic language of the cards to structure the psychic information they receive. The cards provide a framework that organises intuitive impressions into a coherent reading.

Understanding this distinction is useful: the psychic ability comes from the practitioner, not the tool. The tool helps channel and express that ability in a structured way.

Developing Your Own Abilities

One of the lesser-known facts about psychic ability is that it exists on a spectrum. While some individuals are naturally gifted – they receive impressions easily and consistently – most people have some degree of intuitive perception that can be developed.

You may already experience flashes of intuition: knowing who is calling before you check your phone, sensing that something is wrong with a loved one, or having a gut feeling about a decision that proves accurate.

These everyday intuitive experiences are the same faculty that psychics have developed to a higher degree. With practice and attention, many people find that their own intuitive awareness sharpens over time.

A psychic reading can be a catalyst for this development. Experiencing genuine psychic perception firsthand often awakens a curiosity and awareness that supports your own intuitive growth.
