      How the brief is built

      Your text is sent to an AI model, which rewrites it as preparation notes in the style of the reader you choose. The fit meter is a simple keyword estimate that runs in your browser. Nothing is stored on this page.

      What it will not do

      It does not predict events, contact anyone or replace a session. It will not give medical, legal, financial or mental-health advice, and if your text suggests you may be in danger it will point you to real support instead.
